# Bundled reference media

The following clips are shipped as user-facing practice references under the
[Mixkit Stock Video Free License](https://mixkit.co/license/#videoFree). They
contain real recorded human movement; they are not synthetic pose fixtures.

- “Girl dancing happily in a field of flowers” — Mixkit item 4702.
- “Talented urban woman dancing at night on a basketball court” — Mixkit item 51314.
- “Tennis player serving” — Mixkit item 873.
- “Man playing tennis” — Mixkit item 877.

The application performs pose extraction locally in the browser. It does not
transmit, re-host, or analyze YouTube audiovisual content; YouTube results use
the official Data API and privacy-enhanced embedded player.

## Coverage boundary

- Simulated rows test observable constraints; they do not represent a
  population or prove usability for people with the named access needs.
- Screen-reader use, switch access, real low-vision use, real mobility range,
  real Deaf/hard-of-hearing use, and cognitive accessibility remain participant
  or specialist evidence gaps.
- The first real session is Nora's physical phone/TV workflow. Recruiting other
  participants, recording them, or quoting them requires fresh approval.

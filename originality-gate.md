# Originality Gate — Move Canvas

## Market classification

**Crowded but materially differentiated.** Camera-controlled motion play is an
established category. User-authored, AI-structured, calibrated motion games are
the proposed wedge.

## Exact collision result

The original fixed-game concept failed. MoveToPlayGames already documents QR
pairing, on-device pose landmarks, encrypted WebRTC, calibration, seated play,
and TV motion games. Move Canvas must not reproduce that product with different
art.

The revised complete loop is:

`describe → generate bounded blueprint → review/edit movement → calibrate → pair → play → save/replay`

No exact public parity was verified across MoveToPlayGames, Active Arcade,
Catch Flex, PoseBlocks, or the Google Gemini/MediaPipe experiment.

## Removal and agent tests

- Remove generation, review, and calibration: it becomes a fixed-game clone.
- Remove the runtime and pairing: it becomes an AI idea generator.
- Replace the model with a better model: the validated blueprint schema,
  personal calibration, pose controller, deterministic runtime, saved games,
  and replay remain valuable.
- Use a general chatbot: it can suggest rules but cannot complete the calibrated
  playable TV loop.

## What will not be copied

Competitor game names, mechanics combinations, visual identities, onboarding,
copy, scoring, level structures, characters, assets, or navigation. The first
mechanic uses generic collect/dodge primitives derived independently.

## Decision

**`Pass — crowded but differentiated` — 2026-11-19.** Reopen immediately if an
exact prompt-to-calibrated-TV-game product is found or the implementation
reduces authorship to cosmetic preset selection.
